# English integrity and readiness

## Provenance

Keep original prompts, source sentences, official answers, model-derived
examples, user attempts, and feedback as separate records. A reference essay
or translation is not an official answer unless its provenance says so.

## Readiness

English readiness is incomplete when source text, task requirements, meaning
units, logic relations, terminology, or sentence-level evidence is missing.
Do not claim exam readiness from a polished sample alone.

## Ownership

- `learn` builds the shared Subject and persists the domain records;
- `practice` renders writing and translation prompts and separate answer PDFs;
- `review` reads original PDFs and diagnoses eligible user attempts;
- `sort` consumes evidence-backed review priorities;
- `optimize` finds duplicate expressions, oversized templates, fragmented
  cards, and missing graph relations without merging distinct meanings;
- `heal exam` identifies missing writing functions, grammar structures, or
  translation patterns and may record trusted external supplements.

Source, official answer, historical attempt, and Learner State remain
immutable to the domain model.
